TradeDesk AI – Contractor Reply & Estimate Assistant

You just finished a rough estimate on a job site. Now you're staring at an email from a homeowner asking for a formal quote. You know the numbers are right, but typing it all out professionally takes forever. And let's be honest—your thumbs are tired.

TradeDesk AI turns your rough notes into polished replies, quotes, and estimates in seconds. It's a browser extension that works inside the email and forms you already use. No new apps to learn. No data sent to some unknown cloud server. Just fast, professional communication that makes you look good.

The problem with writing quotes and replies

Every contractor knows this pain. You spend hours on a job, then more hours writing it up. Homeowners expect quick, professional responses. But you're not a secretary. You're an electrician, plumber, or HVAC tech.

Typing out scope of work, materials, labor breakdowns, and follow-up details eats into your day. Worse, one typo or missing detail can cost you the job—or worse, land you in a dispute.

TradeDesk AI solves this

TradeDesk AI is built for tradespeople, not office workers. It understands how you talk about your work. It knows the difference between a permit application and a follow-up. And it writes like a professional without sounding like a robot.

Here's what it does: you type a few notes, pick a scenario, and get a complete reply in seconds. You can tweak it or send it as-is. Either way, you're done in under a minute.

Key benefits that matter to contractors

Save 10-15 minutes per reply – That adds up fast. If you send 5 quotes a day, you're saving nearly an hour. That's time for another job, more family time, or just a break.

Never sound unprofessional again – Even if you're typing on your phone between jobs, every reply comes out clean, correct, and consistent. Your business looks bigger than it is.

Your data stays private – TradeDesk AI uses your own API key. No cloud backend holds your quotes, customer info, or pricing. You own everything.

Works where you already work – Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, or any web form. No extra logins, no switching apps. It's just there when you need it.

Tailored to your trade – HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, handyman, and more. The terminology, pricing structure, and document types match your actual work.

How it works (three simple steps)

Step 1: Install the extension – Add TradeDesk AI to Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. It sits quietly in your browser until you need it.

Step 2: Set up your business profile – Enter your company name, license number, insurance info, and typical pricing once. That's it. Every reply auto-fills these details.

Step 3: Start typing – Open any email or web form. Type a few rough notes like "replace water heater, 50 gal, labor $800, permit needed." Click the TradeDesk icon, choose "Quote," and get a complete professional reply ready to send.

FAQ

Is my data safe with TradeDesk AI? Yes. The extension sends your notes directly to the AI provider you choose (OpenAI, Groq, Anthropic, etc.) using your own API key. Nothing is stored on TradeDesk servers. Your quotes, customer info, and pricing stay on your machine.

Can I use it without a paid plan? Yes. The free plan gives you 5 AI replies per day, 5 saved templates, 1 business profile, and 3 scenarios. That's enough to test it on real jobs. Upgrade when you need more.

Does it work on my phone? TradeDesk AI is a browser extension, so it works on any device where you can install extensions. On phones, it works in browsers that support extensions (like Kiwi or Edge on Android). iOS support is limited to Safari extensions.

Which trades does it support? It supports 8 trades with specialized terminology: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, handyman, painting, and general contracting. More trades are added regularly.
