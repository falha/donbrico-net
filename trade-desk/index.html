Frequently Asked Questions

Is my customer data safe with TradeDesk AI?

Yes. All saved details and business profiles are stored in Chrome's local storage on your device. The extension sends notes directly to your chosen AI provider (or runs on-device via Gemini Nano). Nothing is stored on Donbrico servers.

Does it support my specific trade?

Yes. TradeDesk AI is pre-configured with specialized templates and terms for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, painting, handyman, and general contracting.

How does the 'Bring Your Own Key' (BYOK) work?

You can connect your own API key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, or OpenRouter in settings. This gives you ultra-fast, unlimited AI generation and handles large estimates in seconds.
