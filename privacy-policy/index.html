Overview

Last updated: January 2026

Donbrico is committed to protecting your privacy. All Donbrico Chrome and Edge extensions store data locally on your device. This website does not use analytics, cookies, or any tracking scripts.

Donbrico Autofill AI

What data we collect

None. All profile data is stored locally in Chrome's storage on the user's device. No data is transmitted to any server owned or operated by Donbrico.

How profile data is stored

Chrome's storage.local API, never chrome.storage.sync for sensitive data. Data remains on the device and is never uploaded.

Third-party AI providers (Bring Your Own Key)

If the user configures a Bring Your Own Key provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, or OpenRouter, their form context and relevant profile data is sent to that third-party provider. Donbrico is not responsible for the privacy practices of those providers. Gemini Nano (the default AI) runs entirely on-device.

Permissions used

storage (local profile), activeTab (detect forms on current tab), scripting (inject autofill button), host_permissions (work on any website the user visits).

Data deletion

Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data.

Donbrico HostReply AI

What data we collect

None. Your Host Memory property details and settings are stored locally in Chrome's storage on your device. No data is transmitted to Donbrico servers.

How your data is stored

Property details are stored in Chrome's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you use a third-party AI provider.

Third-party AI providers (Bring Your Own Key)

If you configure a Bring Your Own Key provider such as OpenAI, the guest message and your Host Memory details are sent to that third-party provider to generate a response. If you use Gemini Nano (where available), all processing is on-device. Donbrico does not store or log any AI requests.

Permissions used

storage (for Host Memory and settings), activeTab (to detect text fields), scripting (to inject the reply toolbar).

Data deletion

Uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored property details and settings. No data is retained on Donbrico servers.

Donbrico AI Reply Assistant

What data we collect

None. All saved replies and settings are stored locally in Chrome's storage on your device. No data is transmitted to Donbrico servers.

How your data is stored

Saved replies and templates are stored in Chrome's local storage. If you enable Chrome sync, this data may be synced across your devices via Google's servers — that sync is controlled by Chrome, not by this extension.

Third-party AI providers (Bring Your Own Key)

When you use the AI reply generation feature, the text context (the field you're replying to), your selected tone, and any prompt or template variables are sent to your configured AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, or OpenRouter). If you use Gemini Nano (the default), all AI processing occurs on-device and nothing leaves your browser. Donbrico does not store, log, or process any AI requests.

Permissions used

storage (for saved replies and settings), activeTab (to detect text fields), scripting (to inject the reply toolbar), host_permissions (to work on any website you visit).

Data deletion

Uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored replies, templates, and settings. No data is retained on Donbrico servers.

Donbrico WorkDesk

What data we collect

None. Your workspace names, tab URLs, and window arrangements are stored locally in Chrome's storage on your device. No data is transmitted to Donbrico servers.

How your data is stored

All workspace data is stored in Chrome's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you manually export it.

Third-party AI providers (Bring Your Own Key)

When using AI features (naming, cleanup, search), tab titles and URLs may be sent to your configured AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.). If you use Gemini Nano, all processing is on-device. Donbrico does not store or log any AI requests.

Permissions used

storage (for workspaces), tabs (to save/restore sessions), windows (to manage multiple windows), notifications (for save reminders).

Data deletion

Uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored workspaces and settings. No data is retained on Donbrico servers.

Null Carrier — Steam Game

The game itself does not collect personal data. Steam's own privacy policy governs any data collected through the Steam platform.

This Website (donbrico.net)

No analytics, no cookies, no tracking scripts. The website is a static HTML site hosted on Cloudflare Pages. No forms, no user accounts, no data collection of any kind.
