Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work on web-based insurance portals?

Yes. Autofill AI works on any website with standard HTML form fields, including web-based insurance portals and agent management systems.

Is sensitive policyholder data safe?

When using Gemini Nano (Chrome built-in AI), all data stays on your device. No data is ever sent to Donbrico. If you use a third-party API key, data goes to that provider per their privacy policy.

Can I save multiple client profiles?

Pro users can save up to 10 profiles, making it easy to switch between different clients or policy types within a single session.

Does it work on internal company systems?

As long as the system is browser-based and uses standard HTML inputs, yes. Legacy systems with non-standard form elements may have limited support.

Is it compliant with data privacy regulations?

The extension stores data locally on your device and does not transmit data to Donbrico. For regulatory compliance requirements, review your organisation's policies on browser extensions before use.
