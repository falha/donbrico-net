How it works for ecommerce support

1. Build your template library

Save your top 30+ support replies. Tag by category — refunds, shipping, product questions — for fast, accurate retrieval.

2. Open any support ticket

Open a ticket in Gorgias, Zendesk, or Gmail. Click inside the reply field — the AI toolbar appears automatically.

3. Insert or generate

Search saved templates or type a quick prompt. The AI drafts a full response. Review, adjust tone if needed, and send.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work in Gorgias and Zendesk?

Yes. AI Reply Assistant works in any website with a text field, including Gorgias, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Shopify Inbox, and Gmail. No integration required.

Can I maintain brand tone?

Yes. You can save your brand voice as a tone preset and AI will match it when generating responses.

How many templates can I save?

Free plan includes 30 templates. Pro gives you unlimited templates, plus smart variables like {{order_id}} and {{customer_name}}.

Is AI data private?

When using Gemini Nano (Chrome-only), everything stays on-device. If you use a third-party API key, data goes to that provider — never to Donbrico.

Does the team plan exist?

Currently licenses are per-user. Each agent installs their own copy. Team billing is on the roadmap.
