Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work in LinkedIn, Gmail, and our ATS?

Yes. RecruitReply AI works on any website with a text field, including Gmail, LinkedIn, Outlook Web, and most ATS email composers (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday).

How does AI personalise my templates?

When you trigger AI on a saved template, it rewrites the message using context it finds on the current page (candidate name, role title, company) plus any prompt you add. You review and edit before sending.

Can I share templates with my team?

The extension stores templates locally per browser. Export/import as JSON (Pro) lets you back up and share templates with colleagues.

Is candidate data safe?

Yes. All processing stays on-device with Gemini Nano (Chrome). If you use your own API key, data goes only to that provider. Donbrico never sees your messages or candidate data.
