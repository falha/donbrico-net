How it works

1. Save your profile once

Enter your contact details, LinkedIn, work history, education, and skills. Add a bio paragraph the AI can draw from when writing open-ended answers.

2. Visit any job application

Open a role on LinkedIn, Indeed, or go directly to an employer's ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby). The ⚡ button appears when a form is detected.

3. One click, then review

Hit ⚡ to fill standard fields instantly. AI drafts responses to open-ended questions based on your profile. You review everything before submitting — it's always your call.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which job boards and ATS platforms does it support?

Autofill AI detects forms on any website with standard HTML inputs. This includes LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, and most employer career portals.

Can it write cover letters for me?

Yes. When it encounters a cover letter or open-ended question field, AI drafts an answer using your saved profile — your experience, skills, and bio. You always review and edit before submitting.

Can I save different profiles for different types of roles?

Yes — with the Pro plan (up to 10 profiles). For example, one profile tailored for product management roles and another for operations roles, each with different bio emphasis and skill highlights.

Is my personal data safe?

Your profile is stored locally on your device. When using Gemini Nano (Chrome's built-in AI), all AI processing stays on-device — nothing leaves your browser. If you use a third-party API key, data goes to that provider only. Donbrico never stores your data.

Is it free?

The free plan includes unlimited form filling with one profile and local AI via Gemini Nano. Pro ($5.99/month) adds multiple profiles, adaptive learning, and cover letter generation.
