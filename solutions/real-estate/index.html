Frequently Asked Questions

Do these extensions work with Zillow, Realtor.com, and MLS portals?

Autofill AI works on any website with standard HTML forms, including Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, and most MLS portals. AI Reply Assistant works in any text field, including Gmail and Outlook Web.

Is client or property data stored anywhere?

No. All data is stored locally in your browser. AI processing uses Gemini Nano — Chrome's built-in on-device model — so nothing is sent to any server. If you use your own API key, only you control which provider receives data.

Can I save a separate profile for each listing?

Yes. Autofill AI Pro allows up to 10 saved profiles. Create one per major listing or client profile, then select the right one from the popup before filling any form.

Can WorkDesk restore tabs from a previous session?

Yes. WorkDesk saves your open tabs as a named workspace. You can restore any saved workspace at any time, even after closing Chrome. Pro users can also export workspaces and search across them.

Are all three extensions free?

All three have a generous free tier. AI Reply Assistant and Autofill AI unlock advanced features (multiple profiles, smart variables, AI generation) on Pro plans starting at $5.99/month each. WorkDesk Pro removes workspace limits.

Three extensions. One less stressful workday.

All free to install. No account required. Chrome only for now — Edge rolling out soon.
