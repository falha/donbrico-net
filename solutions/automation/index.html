The problem

  • 📝Filling the same information into dozens of different forms every single week
  • 🕐Switching between tabs to copy-paste data into job boards and ATS systems

How Autofill AI solves it

Save your profile once. The extension fills standard fields instantly and uses on-device AI to write custom answers for open-ended questions — entirely in your browser.

  • ✓  Works on any website with HTML forms
  • ✓  AI writes context-aware answers for open-ended fields
  • ✓  Review all AI-generated content before submission

How it works

1. Save your profile

Enter your name, email, LinkedIn, bio, and skills. Stored locally in your browser, never on a server.

2. Visit any form

The extension detects forms automatically. A ⚡ button appears at the top of the form, ready to fill.

3. One click, done

Standard fields fill instantly from your profile. AI generates answers for open-ended questions like cover letters.
