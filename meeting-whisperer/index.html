Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is local transcription?

It depends on your hardware. On modern laptops with dedicated GPUs or neural accelerators, a 1-hour recording typically transcribes in under 5 minutes. Standard CPUs process it in about 15-20 minutes.

What languages are supported?

MeetingWhisperer AI supports over 30 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Portuguese, Chinese, Hindi, Russian, and Korean.

Can it run in the background?

Yes. Simply minimize the app while the local thread processes your audio queue. A desktop notification will alert you when your transcript is ready.
