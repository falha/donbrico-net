ReceiptWhisperer AI — Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

How do I install ReceiptWhisperer AI on my computer?

Download the installer from the Donbrico product page at the provided URL. Run the downloaded file and follow the on-screen prompts—no complex configuration is required. Once installed, launch the application, and you’ll be ready to start scanning receipts immediately.

What file formats are supported for receipt uploads?

ReceiptWhisperer AI supports both image files (such as JPEG and PNG) and PDF documents. You can drag and drop these files directly into the application or use the upload button to select them from your file system.

Do I need an internet connection to use ReceiptWhisperer AI?

No, you do not. All AI and OCR processing happens locally on your machine, so you can scan and organize receipts entirely offline. An internet connection is only required for downloading the software or exporting data to cloud services if you choose.

How do I scan my first receipt?

Open ReceiptWhisperer AI and click the upload button or drag a receipt image or PDF into the designated area. The AI will automatically process the file and extract the merchant name, date, total, and line items within seconds. Review the extracted data, make any corrections if needed, and save it to your organized library.

Features & Usage

How accurate is the OCR for handwritten receipts?

ReceiptWhisperer AI uses advanced AI-based OCR designed to handle both printed and most handwritten text. Accuracy depends on the clarity of the original receipt, but for standard printed receipts, extraction of key fields like totals and dates is highly reliable. For handwritten receipts, results may vary, and you can manually edit any extracted data.

Can I export my receipt data to accounting software?

Yes, you can export your organized receipt data to CSV, PDF, and other common formats. This allows you to import the data into accounting tools like QuickBooks, Excel, or your preferred expense management system. Simply select the receipts you want to export and choose your desired format.

How does smart categorization and tagging work?

ReceiptWhisperer AI automatically analyzes the merchant name and line items to assign a category, such as "Office Supplies" or "Travel." You can also create custom tags to further organize your expenses. These categories and tags are fully editable, so you can adjust them to match your personal or business accounting structure.

Can I scan multiple receipts at once?

Yes, you can upload multiple receipt images or PDFs in a single batch. ReceiptWhisperer AI will process each file individually and display the extracted data in a queue. This is especially useful for freelancers or travelers who need to process several receipts after a trip.

Does the app recognize line items from grocery or retail receipts?

Yes, the AI is trained to extract individual line items, including product names, quantities, and prices. For complex receipts with many items, the extracted data may require a quick review, but the vast majority of line items are captured correctly. This feature is particularly helpful for expense-conscious individuals tracking detailed spending.

Privacy & Security

Is my financial data stored on cloud servers?

No. ReceiptWhisperer AI processes all data locally on your machine, meaning your receipt images and extracted information never leave your computer. This ensures your financial data remains completely private and under your control.

Does the AI send my receipt data to third-party services?

No, the AI does not send any data to third-party services. All OCR and data extraction are performed locally using the application’s built-in models. The only time data leaves your machine is when you manually choose to export a file or save it to an external service.

Can I use ReceiptWhisperer AI on a company computer with strict IT policies?

Yes, because the application runs entirely offline and does not require cloud connectivity, it is suitable for use on company-managed devices. However, you should verify your organization’s software installation policies before installing any desktop application. No data is transmitted over the network during normal use.

How is my data protected if I lose my laptop?

Since all data is stored locally on your machine, your receipt information is only as secure as your device’s own security measures. We recommend using full-disk encryption and regular backups to protect your data. ReceiptWhisperer AI itself does not store any data externally.

The free tier of ReceiptWhisperer AI includes basic scanning capabilities and allows you to process up to 10 receipts per month. You still get full AI-powered OCR extraction and local processing, but with a monthly cap. This is ideal for occasional users or those who want to test the software before committing.

Troubleshooting

Why is ReceiptWhisperer AI not recognizing text from my receipt?

First, ensure your receipt image is clear, well-lit, and not blurry. Low-quality scans or heavily crumpled receipts can reduce OCR accuracy. If the issue persists, try converting the file to a different format (e.g., from PDF to PNG) and re-uploading. You can also manually edit the extracted fields if the AI misreads any data.

The application is running slowly when processing multiple receipts. What can I do?

Batch processing large numbers of high-resolution images can be resource-intensive. Close other applications to free up system memory, and consider resizing very large images before uploading. ReceiptWhisperer AI is optimized for local processing, so performance depends on your computer’s CPU and RAM.

I accidentally deleted a receipt. Can I recover it?

Receipts are stored locally in the application’s database, and there is no built-in recycle bin or undo feature for deletions. We strongly recommend regularly exporting your data to CSV or PDF as a backup. If you haven’t exported, the deleted receipt may be unrecoverable.

The app won’t launch after installation. What should I do?

Make sure your computer meets the minimum system requirements listed on the Donbrico product page. Try running the application as an administrator (right-click and select "Run as administrator") if you’re on Windows. If it still won’t launch, reinstall the application or contact support through the Donbrico website for further assistance.
