Frequently Asked Questions

Is my bank information safe?

Yes. ReceiptWhisperer AI doesn't connect to bank APIs or sync with external servers. It reads documents and scans locally, leaving you in 100% control of your personal bank data.

How does the OCR function?

It runs an optimized offline Tesseract and CNN pipeline built directly into the application runtime, enabling zero-latency recognition without cloud delays.

Can I use it for tax audits?

Absolutely. Exported records include precise file links to matching receipt scans stored in your local directory, serving as clean, self-contained documentation for audits.
