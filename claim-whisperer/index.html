Frequently Asked Questions

Does ClaimWhisperer upload my claim documents to the cloud?

No. ClaimWhisperer runs entirely on your Windows PC. Your photos, receipts, PDFs, OCR text, extracted item data, proof links, notes, values, and exports never leave your computer. The app does not require an account, connect to insurer portals, sync projects, or submit claims.

Does ClaimWhisperer submit my claim or connect to my insurer?

No — by design. ClaimWhisperer is a proof organizer and export tool. It produces files you can send to your insurer, adjuster, landlord, or records folder yourself. It does not submit claims, connect to insurer portals, interpret policy coverage, or provide insurance advice. Always review your packet before sharing it.

What file types can I import?

ClaimWhisperer accepts JPG, PNG, PDF, TXT, MD, CSV, and HTML files. You can drop individual files or entire folders. Unsupported files are skipped with a visible reason and your original files are never modified — all processing uses app-owned copies.

Can one receipt support multiple items?

Yes. Proof linking in ClaimWhisperer is many-to-many: one receipt can be linked to multiple items, and one item can have multiple proof files. You can attach, detach, and reuse proof files from the review screen. The proof ZIP and audit manifest reflect every link you've set.

What does the export packet contain?

The full export bundle includes: a claim packet PDF with cover page, project summary, room-grouped items, proof references, and disclaimer; an insurer-friendly summary PDF; an inventory CSV and XLSX with all item fields and totals; a proof ZIP with organized source copies grouped by room; and an audit manifest JSON listing every file and its hash. Missing values are shown as blank or "review needed" — nothing is invented.

What languages are supported?

ClaimWhisperer supports nine languages for the app interface and localized export labels: English (en-US), French (fr-FR), Spanish (es-ES and es-419), German (de-DE), Italian (it-IT), Portuguese Brazilian (pt-BR), Japanese (ja-JP), and Chinese Simplified (zh-CN). You can switch language in Settings without restarting.

Is ClaimWhisperer a subscription?

No. ClaimWhisperer is a one-time purchase at USD 49.99 (USD 29.99 during the launch offer for the first 60 days or first 100 purchases). The 15-day Microsoft Store trial is full-feature — no file limits, no watermarks, no restricted exports. Try it with your real proof and review a real export before buying.
