Frequently Asked Questions

Does ClaimWhisperer upload my claim documents to the cloud?

No. ClaimWhisperer runs on your Windows PC. Your photos, receipts, PDFs, OCR text, extracted item data, proof links, notes, values, identifier reviews, redaction choices, and exports stay on your computer. The app does not require an account, connect to insurer portals, sync projects, or submit claims.

Does ClaimWhisperer submit my claim or connect to my insurer?

No - by design. ClaimWhisperer is a proof organizer and export tool. It produces files you can send yourself. It does not submit claims, connect to insurer portals, interpret policy coverage, provide insurance advice, estimate official replacement value, calculate official depreciation, predict payouts, or guarantee acceptance.

What starter templates are included?

You can start blank or choose apartment theft, basement water damage, moving damage, electronics warranty, or pre-loss inventory. Templates create project rooms and starter notes only. They do not import files, run extraction automatically, or provide insurer-specific workflows.

Can it suggest barcodes, serial numbers, or model numbers?

Yes. ClaimWhisperer can detect local barcode, serial, and model candidates from file names and extracted text. You review each candidate, see its source, and choose whether to accept, reject, or edit it before it updates an item.

What does possible similar-photo review do?

It groups exact duplicates and possible similar photos using conservative local signals so you can inspect messy imports. ClaimWhisperer does not delete, merge, exclude, or link files automatically.

Are the packet styles insurer-specific templates?

No. The compact adjuster summary, room-by-room packet, and proof-heavy packet are generic export styles. They organize your reviewed data differently, but they do not promise that any insurer, adjuster, landlord, warranty provider, or platform will accept the packet.

Are redactions automatic or complete?

No. Redaction suggestions are local review aids for generated output text. Only suggestions you approve are applied. Source photos, PDFs, receipts, and other proof files copied into a proof ZIP remain original and must be reviewed before sharing.

What file types can I import?

ClaimWhisperer accepts JPG, PNG, PDF, TXT, MD, CSV, and HTML files. You can drop individual files or entire folders. Unsupported files are skipped with a visible reason and your original files are never modified - all processing uses app-owned copies.

What does the export bundle contain?

The full export bundle includes a packet PDF, summary PDF, inventory CSV and XLSX, proof ZIP with organized source copies, and audit manifest JSON. Missing values are shown as blank or marked for review rather than invented.

Is ClaimWhisperer a subscription?

No. ClaimWhisperer is a one-time purchase at USD 49.99 (USD 29.99 during the launch offer for the first 60 days or first 100 purchases). The 15-day Microsoft Store trial is full-feature - no file limits, no watermarks, no restricted exports. Try it with your own proof and review a real export before buying.
