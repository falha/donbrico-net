FAQ

Is patient data stored securely?

Yes. All saved profile data is stored in Chrome's local storage on your device. It is never sent to Donbrico's servers. When using Gemini Nano (Chrome's built-in AI), everything stays entirely local.

Does it work on Availity, CoverMyMeds, and payer portals?

MedAutoFill is designed to work on standard web forms including payer portals, prior auth platforms, credentialing sites, and EHR web portals. Compatibility varies by website structure and form implementation.

What if a form uses unusual field labels?

MedAutoFill uses AI-assisted matching to recognize label variations — such as "Member Information", "Rendering Provider", or "Subscriber". If it cannot confidently match a field, it leaves it empty for you to fill manually.

Is this HIPAA compliant?

MedAutoFill stores data locally on your device. When using local AI (Gemini Nano), no data leaves your browser. If you enable a cloud AI provider, data is sent to them per their policies. Your organization's compliance team should evaluate usage for your specific requirements.
