Frequently Asked Questions

Is my customer data safe with SellerDesk AI?

Yes. All saved details and business profiles are stored in Chrome's local storage on your device. The extension sends notes directly to your chosen AI provider (or runs on-device via Gemini Nano). Nothing is stored on Donbrico servers.

Does it support my specific marketplace?

Yes. SellerDesk AI is built to work seamlessly with Etsy, eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Shopify, Mercari, Poshmark, as well as regular email providers like Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo Mail.

How does the 'Bring Your Own Key' (BYOK) work?

You can connect your own API key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, or OpenRouter in settings. This gives you ultra-fast, unlimited AI generation and ensures you have the best quality replies for your customers.
