FULL DESCRIPTION

Stop losing time typing the same responses to buyer messages across Etsy, eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Shopify, and more.

Every minute you spend drafting replies to "Is this still available?" or "Can you ship to Canada?" is time you're not listing products, fulfilling orders, or growing your business. SellerDesk AI gives you back that time — generating professional, personalized replies in seconds, directly inside your browser.

✅ Reply in seconds, not minutes — AI reads the buyer's message and your store policies, then drafts a complete response. One click to send. ✅ Works across 8+ platforms — Etsy, eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Shopify, Mercari, Poshmark, Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail. One tool replaces separate workflows. ✅ Always on-brand — Customize tone (friendly, professional, urgent) and scenarios (shipping questions, price negotiation, return requests). Your voice stays consistent. ✅ No more copy-paste errors — Saved reply library stores your best responses. Premium users get unlimited storage. Edit once, use everywhere. ✅ Context-aware personalization — Integrates property/listing details so replies include specific item names, prices, and policies automatically. ✅ Keyboard shortcuts for power users — Alt+Shift+G (generate), S (saved replies), M (message menu), Z (undo). Move faster than clicking.

How it works:

  1. Install the extension — Works in Chrome. No account needed to start. Open any buyer message on supported platforms.
  2. Click the SellerDesk AI icon — The extension reads the message context. Choose a saved reply or click "Generate AI Reply."
  3. Review and send — AI drafts a response based on your store policies and product details. Edit if needed, then send. That's it.

  • 1 property profile (for personalized listing details)
  • Full access across all supported platforms
