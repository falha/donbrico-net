ABOUT DONBRICO

Built to remove the repetitive
parts of professional work

Donbrico builds AI browser extensions for professionals who want to move faster without sacrificing quality.

Mission

Every professional has workflows that are mostly repetitive. Filling the same forms. Writing near-identical replies. Rebuilding the same research session.

These tasks aren't the job — they're the overhead of the job. Donbrico builds browser extensions that automate the overhead, so the work that actually matters gets more of your attention.

⚡

Autofill AI

Fills any web form from a saved profile. AI handles open-ended questions like cover letters and summaries.

✉️

AI Reply Assistant

Saves reply templates and generates AI responses in any text field — Gmail, LinkedIn, helpdesks, and more.

🗂️

WorkDesk

Saves and restores browser workspaces. AI names and organizes sessions automatically.

Privacy stance

Chrome

All three extensions default to Gemini Nano — Google's on-device AI model built into Chrome. AI runs entirely on your machine: no API calls, no data leaving your browser. Your profile, your replies, your tabs — all stored locally.

Edge (coming soon)

Microsoft Edge does not support on-device Gemini Nano. Edge users can connect their own API key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, or OpenRouter) to unlock AI features. All other functionality works identically.

Donbrico never stores your data. Never.

Solo-built

Donbrico is a solo operation. Every line of code, every design decision, every support reply — one person. That means the products stay lean, focused, and honest. No features added to hit metrics. No roadmap driven by investor pressure.

There's also a game in the works: Null Carrier — a deep-space signal puzzle, something completely different from the extensions.
