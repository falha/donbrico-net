FAQ

Is my client or property data safe?

Completely. All saved drafts and agent profiles are stored locally in Chrome's storage on your device. The default Gemini Nano AI runs entirely on-device with zero data leaving your machine.

Does it support Centris and Realtor.ca?

Yes! PropertyFill AI is custom-tailored with specialized adapters for Centris.ca (supporting bilingual English/French fields) and Realtor.ca/RealtorLink.

Do I need a paid plan to use my own AI key?

No. Bringing your own API key (BYOK) from OpenAI, Claude, Groq, or OpenRouter is fully supported on the Free plan.

What platforms are supported?

Out of the box, it supports Centris, Realtor.ca, Zillow, Flexmls, and Matrix portals. We are continually adding support for more MLS platforms.
