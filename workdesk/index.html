Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work with tab groups?

Yes. WorkDesk fully supports Chrome tab groups. When you restore a workspace, your groups are recreated exactly as they were.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Your workspace names, tab URLs, and window arrangements are stored locally in Chrome's storage. When using Gemini Nano, everything stays on your device.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free includes up to 5 workspaces, AI naming, and archiving. Pro removes all limits: unlimited workspaces, AI cleanup, semantic search, and multi-window support.

Can I export my workspaces?

Yes. Pro users can export their entire library as JSON (for backups), CSV (for spreadsheets), or HTML Bookmarks.
