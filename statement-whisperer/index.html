Frequently Asked Questions

Does it upload my statements?

No. StatementWhisperer processes statement documents locally on your Windows PC.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. It converts documents you import. It does not use bank logins, Plaid, or online sync.

What can it export?

Generic CSV, XLSX, QuickBooks CSV when enabled, a review bundle, and optional QBO/OFX/QIF files when the Accounting Export Pack is enabled and acknowledged.

Are QBO, OFX, and QIF guaranteed to import everywhere?

No. Accounting importers vary. Those formats stay off by default and should be tested with sample files first.

Does it replace accounting review?

No. It helps convert and review statement rows. You should inspect the output before giving it to an accountant or importing it elsewhere.

Keep financial documents on your PC.
